Prepared September 14, 2026. **Not published or effective yet.** Proposed URL: https://tssprint.com/terms. No customer cancellation fee, refund deadline, liability waiver or dispute venue has been invented in this draft.

## Proposed customer-facing copy

### Using the website

The Sticker Smith provides tssprint.com for browsing print services, configuring products, uploading artwork, requesting quotes and placing orders. You may use these features for lawful personal or business purposes. Do not interfere with the website, attempt unauthorized access, or submit material you do not have the right to use. Contact thestickersmith@gmail.com or (510) 634-8203 for assistance.

### Product selections, prices and quotes

Review the product, size, finish, number of pieces, number of print batches, add-ons, delivery choice and total before paying. A batch quantity of one means one order of the piece count shown. Quotes for custom jobs must be approved before invoicing. Promotional codes are subject to their displayed eligibility conditions and are checked by the server.

### Artwork and production proofs

You retain your rights in artwork you submit. You authorize us to use that artwork to review, prepare proofs and fulfill your requested job. Supplying artwork does not by itself authorize publication in our portfolio.

A website preview helps you configure a product; it is not final production approval. Review the production proof for spelling, size, layout, orientation and cut lines. Reply with approval or requested changes. Production begins after payment and required artwork/proof approval. If you send artwork later or request design help, that work must be completed before production can start.

### Payment

Available payment options are shown at checkout or on your approved invoice. The named payment provider handles payment entry and its own payment terms. An invoice being created or opened does not mean it has been paid. Keep the order and payment reference. Contact us if you believe you were charged but did not receive an order confirmation.

### Turnaround, shipping and pickup

Production estimates begin after required payment, usable artwork and proof approval. A requested deadline is not a confirmed deadline until the shop agrees to it. Shipping transit is separate from production. For pickup, wait for a ready-for-pickup notice before visiting 23673 Connecticut St, Hayward, CA 94545.

### Changes, cancellations and problems

Contact us promptly with your order reference if you need a change or cancellation, or if work arrives damaged or does not match the approved proof. Include photographs when helpful. The shop will review the order and explain available next steps.

### Accounts and connected services

Keep your account access secure. Shop administrators may connect or disconnect QuickBooks through the admin page. QuickBooks, PayPal and other third-party services have their own terms. Disconnecting an accounting integration does not remove existing accounting records or cancel customer orders.

### Questions and updates

Questions about these terms can be sent to thestickersmith@gmail.com. We will update the effective date when these terms change. Any change to an already accepted order requires an appropriate agreement or notice; a page update alone should not be treated as permission to alter a paid job.
