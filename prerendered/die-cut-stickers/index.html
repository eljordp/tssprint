Questions

Die-Cut Stickers FAQ

Can you cut around any shape?

Yes, as long as the artwork has enough clean edge space for a safe cut path. We set up the cut line during proofing and send it for approval before printing.

Are die-cut stickers waterproof?

Our standard vinyl die-cut stickers are waterproof and built for indoor or outdoor use. Lamination and material choice affect final durability.
